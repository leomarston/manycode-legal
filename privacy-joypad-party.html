Privacy Policy — Joypad Party

For the app Joypad Party (on the App Store as “Party Games - Joypad Party”) on Apple TV, iPad, iPhone and Mac.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Joypad Party (the “App”), published by Özcan Sarıdoğan under the Crystal Nation name. It explains what the App stores and what it sends.

Usage statistics

The App sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog, our analytics provider (servers in the European Union), so we can see how many people play and which games they play. The statistics cover:

  • the App being opened, and a TV, iPad or Mac showing its connect screen (with a running count of that screen’s game sessions, so the events of one session can be grouped);
  • a phone, game controller or Siri Remote joining, with the number of players and the kind of phone; on a phone, how it joined (from the list, by code or with the QR code) and the kind of screen;
  • a game starting, a round reaching its results, or a game ending: which game, the number of players, how long it lasted, how many rounds were finished and how it ended (for example back to the games list, or everyone left);
  • the “get the app” QR code being shown, and which game store tile was chosen;
  • a game asking for more phones before it can start: which game, how many phones it needed and how many were connected, and, if enough phones joined, how many seconds that took;
  • after a game, the optional “How was …?” question being shown, and each phone’s answer to it: the game, the answer (Loved it, OK, Not good or skipped), the chips picked (such as “Laggy” or “Too hard”), the number of players in the room and the kind of phone.

They include basic device information (device type and model, system and App version, language and region, screen size, time zone) and a random ID that PostHog’s software creates for the App. PostHog receives your device’s IP address when the statistics are uploaded, as with any internet service. We use the statistics to understand and improve the App.

“Send us a note” on that question opens your own mail app with an email to our address (its subject names the game, the App version and the device model); it reaches us like any other email.

Switching it off: “Share anonymous usage data” is on by default and can be switched off on each device: on a phone in the App’s menu, and on Apple TV, iPad and Mac with the “Usage data” tile in the store’s About row.

Data stored on your devices

On your phone, the App saves the name and colour you choose, your favourite and recently played games, your play statistics, the last screen you joined, a random ID that lets a screen recognise your phone when it reconnects, and your settings. On the Apple TV, iPad or Mac, it saves game progress and settings. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Your local network

To play, your phone and your Apple TV, iPad or Mac find each other with Apple’s Bonjour on your Wi-Fi and connect directly. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the system asks for the “Local Network” permission the first time; you can change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network (on a Mac, System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). Over that connection the phone sends the screen your player name, colour, random ID, App version and controls, the text you type in a game (a Drawshow guess) and your answer to “How was …?”, and the screen sends the phones the game state and its device name (for example “Apple TV”). Other people on the same Wi-Fi who open the App can see the screen’s name and join a game.

Purchases and ads

The App has no account, no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no advertising.

Ratings

The App may show Apple’s standard rating prompt. Apple handles that prompt; we see only the public ratings and reviews people choose to post.

Children

The App is a general-audience party game and is not directed specifically to children.

Your choices

You can switch off the usage statistics at any time as described above, and delete the App’s saved data by deleting the App. For any question or request, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
