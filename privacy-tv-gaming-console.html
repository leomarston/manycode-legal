Privacy Policy — TV Gaming Console

For the app TV Gaming Console on Apple TV, iPad and iPhone. The policy for our other apps is here.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to TV Gaming Console (the “App”), published by Emine Sarıdoğan under the Manycode name. The short version: the App collects no data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we have no server that could receive anything from it.

Information we do not collect

The App does not ask you to sign up or sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and we do not collect anything about how you use the App. The App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your devices

On your phone, the App saves the nickname and colour you choose, a randomly generated ID that lets a TV recognise your phone when it reconnects, and your settings. On the TV or iPad that runs the games, it saves game progress (for example the cash and cars you have in Latenight Streets, and the questions Showquiz asked recently, so it does not repeat them) and your settings. This data stays on those devices. Deleting the App deletes it.

Your local network

To play, your phone and your TV or iPad have to find each other. The App uses Apple’s Bonjour on your own Wi-Fi network for that, then connects the devices directly. On iPhone and iPad, iOS asks for your permission (“Local Network”) the first time; you can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Over that local connection, the phone sends the screen your nickname, your colour, its random ID, the App’s version and your controls (button presses, and the steering angle when you steer by tilting the phone). The screen sends the phones the game state and its device name as the system reports it (for example “Apple TV”), so you can pick the right screen. All of this stays inside your home network: none of it is sent to the internet, to us or to anyone else. Other people on the same Wi-Fi network who also open the App can see the screen’s name and join a game, as in any living-room console.

Game controllers and the Siri Remote

If you play with a game controller or the Siri Remote, the App reads its buttons through Apple’s GameController framework to play the game. Nothing about the controller is stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases and ads

The App is free. It has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising.

Ratings

The App may ask you once, through Apple’s standard rating prompt, whether you would like to rate it. Apple handles that prompt; we receive only the public rating and review you choose to post.

Children

The App is a general-audience party game. It is not directed specifically to children, and because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Everything the App saves lives only on your own devices, so you can delete it at any time by deleting the App. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact
