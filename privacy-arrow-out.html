Privacy Policy — Arrow Out

For the game Arrow Out. The policy for our other apps is here.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Arrow Out (the “Game”), published by Emine Sarıdoğan. The short version: your progress stays on your device, the Game shows no ads, and the only data that leaves your device is what Apple, RevenueCat and Meta need for purchases and for measuring our own adverts — described below.

Information we do not collect

The Game does not require an account and does not ask you to sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or microphone. We have no servers of our own that receive data from the Game.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, lives), the name you choose for your profile, your event progress and your settings are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the Game removes this data from your device.

Advertising and attribution (Meta)

We advertise Arrow Out on Facebook and Instagram. To tell which adverts bring players to the Game, the Game includes the Meta (Facebook) SDK. It is used for measuring our own adverts and nothing else. The Game itself shows no adverts.

When you use the Game, the SDK sends Meta a small set of app events, together with a random identifier and basic device information:

  • that the Game was opened;
  • that the tutorial was finished;
  • that a level was won, and its number;
  • that a coin pack or bundle was bought, with its price and currency.

The advertising identifier is off until you say yes. After the tutorial levels, the Game asks once, through Apple's own App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking. Until you tap “Allow”, the SDK does not collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you allow it, Meta also receives the advertising identifier and may link these events to your Meta account. If you decline, the Game works exactly the same — no feature is withheld.

Whatever you choose, Apple may send Meta an anonymous SKAdNetwork notice that an install happened. That is Apple's own mechanism and it carries no identifier for you. Meta's handling of the data it receives is governed by Meta's privacy policy.

You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone.

Purchases

Coin packs and bundles are optional one-time purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. To record purchases, the Game uses RevenueCat, Inc., which processes a randomly generated, anonymous app-user identifier together with the App Store purchase record. This is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game can remind you about events and full lives. These are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not operate push servers.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Your progress lives only on your device, so you can delete it at any time by deleting the Game. We hold no personal data about you on our side. For data Meta or RevenueCat received, see their policies above; you can also stop the advertising identifier at any time in the iPhone Settings app as described.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact
