Getting started

Open Joypad Party on your Apple TV, iPad or Mac: that is the screen. Open the same app on your iPhone: that is the controller. Put both on the same Wi-Fi network. The phone finds the screen by itself; if it doesn’t, tap Type the TV’s code on the phone and type the code shown on the screen.

My phone can’t find the TV

  • Check that the phone and the Apple TV, iPad or Mac are on the same Wi-Fi network. No internet connection is needed.
  • On iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure Joypad Party is on. On an iPad used as the screen, check the same switch on the iPad; on a Mac, in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • Some guest, hotel and office networks keep devices apart. Use a home network, or a personal hotspot both devices join.

How many people can play?

One or two players, each on their own phone.

Can I play with the Siri Remote?

Yes, in some games. On Apple TV, the Siri Remote can browse the games and play the puzzle game and the quiz: press the clickpad on the first screen to join. The racing game needs a phone or a game controller to steer.

Why can’t I see the racing game?

The racing game needs an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later) or an iPad with the A12 Bionic chip or later; it is not offered on older models. Every Mac the App installs on (Apple silicon) runs it.

Does it cost anything?

No. The App is free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your own devices. See the Privacy Policy.
