Email us

The fastest way to get help is email. We usually reply within 2–3 business days. On the phone, Help & tips › Contact support in the App’s menu opens the same address; on a Mac, Help › Contact support.

anycodeapps@gmail.com

To help us quickly, please include your Apple TV, iPad or Mac model, your phone model, their software versions, and what happened.

Getting started

Open Joypad Party on your Apple TV, iPad or Mac: that is the screen. Open the same app on your iPhone: that is the controller. Put both on the same Wi-Fi network. The phone finds the screen by itself; if it doesn’t, tap Type the TV’s code on the phone and type the code shown on the screen.

My phone can’t find the TV

  • Check that the phone and the Apple TV, iPad or Mac are on the same Wi-Fi network. No internet connection is needed.
  • On iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure Joypad Party is on. On an iPad used as the screen, check the same switch on the iPad; on a Mac, in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • Some guest, hotel and office networks keep devices apart. Use a home network, or a personal hotspot both devices join.

How many people can play?

Each game has its own number of players, each on their own phone: Hexcast 1–2, Showquiz 1–16, Latenight Streets 1–4, Final Watch 1–4, Drawshow 3–16, Pickoff 1–20, Scribblegram 3–20, Tumbledown 1–4, Lamplight Canteen 1–4, Talkdown 2–4, Boardwalk Bits 1–4, Snowbluff 3–16, Haute Coop 2–8. Phones beyond a game’s seats watch. Without Joypad Pro, up to 2 players can play (see below).

Can I play with the Siri Remote?

Yes, in some games. On Apple TV, the Siri Remote can browse the games and play Hexcast and Showquiz: press the clickpad on the first screen to join. The other games need a phone; Latenight Streets and Final Watch also play with a game controller.

Why can’t I see Latenight Streets or Final Watch?

They need an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later) or an iPad with the A12 Bionic chip or later, and are not offered on older models. Every Mac the App installs on (Apple silicon) runs them. Final Watch also needs tvOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26, and downloads its game files the first time you open it.

What is free, and what is Joypad Pro?

Without Joypad Pro, up to 2 players can play, 3 games a day are free, and Latenight Streets is always free. Free rooms take a short break between some games. Joypad Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly: it unlocks every game and any number of players for everyone in the room, with no breaks. One player with Joypad Pro unlocks the whole room.

How do I get Joypad Pro?

On your iPhone or iPad: open Joypad Party and tap Get Joypad Pro. Apple TV and Mac don’t sell it; they show where to get it. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. An Apple TV, iPad or Mac signed in with the same Apple Account also has your Joypad Pro.

How do I cancel Joypad Pro?

Apple manages the subscription. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions (or, in the App Store app, tap your picture, then Subscriptions). It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. For a refund, use Apple’s reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore Purchases

Restore Purchases is on every Joypad Pro page and in the phone’s menu, and on Apple TV, iPad and Mac under About › Joypad Pro. It checks the Apple Account signed in on that device.

Gold and the season pass in Latenight Streets

Gold, the starter pack and the season passes are optional purchases made on your phone. They belong to the player on that phone and are kept in the phone’s Keychain: they stay when you delete and reinstall the App. Gold stays on that phone and never expires; season passes come back on another iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple Account with Restore Purchases. If something you bought is missing, email us.

Are there ads?

No advertising from anyone else. The breaks in free rooms show our own Joypad Pro.

What is “How was …?” after a game?

After some games, the screen and each phone ask how the game was: tap Loved it, OK or Not good, pick a chip if you like (such as “Perfect” or “Laggy”), then Send. It is optional: skip it, or it closes by itself. Your answer helps us fix and improve the games. Send us a note opens your mail app with an email to us; its subject already names the game, the App version and your device model.

Where is my data stored?

Your player name, colour, game progress and settings stay on your own devices, and your Latenight Streets wallet stays in your phone’s Keychain. Anonymous usage statistics, with the answers to “How was …?”, and the purchase records kept by RevenueCat are described in the Privacy Policy.
