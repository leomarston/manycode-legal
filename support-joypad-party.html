Getting started

Open Joypad Party on your Apple TV, iPad or Mac: that is the screen. Open the same app on your iPhone: that is the controller. Put both on the same Wi-Fi network. The phone finds the screen by itself; if it doesn’t, tap Type the TV’s code on the phone and type the code shown on the screen.

My phone can’t find the TV

  • Check that the phone and the Apple TV, iPad or Mac are on the same Wi-Fi network. No internet connection is needed.
  • On iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and make sure Joypad Party is on. On an iPad used as the screen, check the same switch on the iPad; on a Mac, in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • Some guest, hotel and office networks keep devices apart. Use a home network, or a personal hotspot both devices join.

How many people can play?

Each game has its own number of players, each on their own phone: Hexcast 1–2, Showquiz 1–16, Latenight Streets 1–2, Final Watch 1–2, Drawshow 3–16, Moonhowl 5–20, Pickoff 1–20, Scribblegram 3–20. Phones beyond a game’s seats watch.

Can I play with the Siri Remote?

Yes, in some games. On Apple TV, the Siri Remote can browse the games and play Hexcast and Showquiz: press the clickpad on the first screen to join. The other games need a phone; Latenight Streets and Final Watch also play with a game controller.

Why can’t I see Latenight Streets or Final Watch?

They need an Apple TV 4K (2nd generation or later) or an iPad with the A12 Bionic chip or later, and are not offered on older models. Every Mac the App installs on (Apple silicon) runs them. Final Watch also needs tvOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26, and downloads its game files the first time you open it.

Are there ads or anything to buy?

The App has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and no ads.

What is “How was …?” after a game?

After some games, the screen and each phone ask how the game was: tap Loved it, OK or Not good, pick a chip if you like (such as “Perfect” or “Laggy”), then Send. It is optional: skip it, or it closes by itself. Your answer helps us fix and improve the games. Send us a note opens your mail app with an email to us; its subject already names the game, the App version and your device model.

Where is my data stored?

Your player name, colour, game progress and settings stay on your own devices. Anonymous usage statistics, with the answers to “How was …?”, are described in the Privacy Policy.
